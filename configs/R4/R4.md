**Autonomous System (AS):** 2121  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture

Router **R4** serves as a core Backbone Router and external gateway for **AS 2121**:

1. **OSPF Backbone (Area 0):** Operates within Area 0, maintaining internal IGP adjacencies with R3 (`121.1.0.9`).
2. **eBGP Peering with AS 2123:** Connects directly to external router **ASBR-2123** via IPv4 (`121.1.0.18`) and IPv6 (`2123:4561:0:5::2`).
3. **Traffic Engineering (AS Path Prepending):** Applies route map `PREPEND-OUT` (`set as-path prepend 2121 2121 2121`) on outbound eBGP updates to AS 2123 to deprioritize AS 2121 as a transit path.
4. **iBGP Connection:** Connects to Route Reflector R3 (`121.0.0.3`) for internal BGP routing synchronization.

- **Networks in Area 0:** `121.0.0.4/32`, `121.1.0.8/30`.
- **Passive Interface:** `Loopback0`.
