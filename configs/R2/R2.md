---

## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture

Router **R2** operates as a **Provider Edge (PE)** router within **AS 2121**, connecting branch offices to the core network:

1. **MPLS L3VPN Provider Edge (PE):** Configured with VRF `ENTERPRISE` (`RD 2121:2`, `RT 2121:999`), servicing the **CE-BR** customer site.
2. **PE-CE Routing (RIPv2):** Uses RIPv2 within VRF `ENTERPRISE` to exchange routes with CE-BR, redistributing RIP into MP-BGP and vice versa.
3. **OSPF Stub Router:** Belongs strictly to OSPF Area 2 (Stub Area), maintaining IPv4 and IPv6 OSPF adjacencies with R1 (`121.1.0.5`).
4. **iBGP / MP-BGP Peering:** Establishes iBGP/VPNv4 sessions with Route Reflector **R3** (`121.0.0.3` / `2123:4561:0:F::3`).
