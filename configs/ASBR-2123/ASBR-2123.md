## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture

Router **ASBR-2123** serves as the border edge router for **Autonomous System 2123 (AS 2123)**:

1. **eBGP External Peering:** Establishes dual-stack eBGP sessions (IPv4 and IPv6) with router **R4** in neighboring **AS 2121**.
2. **Local Prefix Origination:** Injects AS 2123's loopback blocks (`123.0.0.1/32` IPv4 and `2123:4563:0:F::1/128` IPv6) into BGP updates.
3. **Domain Isolation:** Operates as a standalone ASBR without IGP protocols or MPLS running on its WAN interface.
